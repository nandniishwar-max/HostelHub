About HostelHub

A simple digital platform for managing hostel room information and applications.

Our Hostel

HostelHub helps students explore available rooms, view room details, and submit hostel applications through a simple online interface.

Our Purpose

The goal of HostelHub is to make the hostel application process easier by bringing room information and applications together in one place.

What You Can Do

Explore rooms, check availability, view facilities, submit applications, and track application status.
